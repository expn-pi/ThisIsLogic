# Visuals design

- The table's look comes from research the author did with Claude, recorded with its sources and contrast measurements in a claude.ai doc of his: https://claude.ai/artifact/9ozjdkbWXDZAy9CaH328e9. It covers the colors, the font's weight, post-processing, the table, the boards' backgrounds, the blocks' outline and the panel's style. The goal is a better look, not exact colors on screen (see the colors set aside, below).
- Colors are written in sRGB, as hexadecimal `FColor` values, the form that image editors and the research use, and converted with `FLinearColor(FColor)`, which turns them into the linear values that the Color parameter of `M_Block` (Unlit) takes.
- The colors: the table #1B1F25; the boards' backgrounds #2A3039, one color for every board and for the indicators' background; the operators sky blue #5EB8EA, close to one of the research's options the author liked; the parentheses gray #B4BBC4, apart from the other operators, so the groups show at a glance; the propositions yellow #F2CC4D; black text on every block; an outline on every block, near-black (#0D0F12), or white when the block is selected; and the indicators' circles cyan #22D3EE, red #EF4444 or gray #8B949E, provisional, with black symbols and white labels (see `Docs/Indicators.md`).
- The author prefers yellow for the propositions to the orange #E69F00 that the research picked, and a muted yellow, since pure yellow (#FFFF02, tried first) outshone the blue.
- Black text has a contrast ratio (WCAG) of 13.5:1 on the propositions' yellow, 10.8:1 on the parentheses' gray and 9.5:1 on the operators' blue, computed from the code's colors. The indicators' black symbols have 11.6:1 on the cyan, 5.6:1 on the red and 6.8:1 on the gray, and their white labels 13.3:1 on their background. The panel's white text has at least the 4.5:1 agreed for it: 10.2:1 on Normal, 4.8:1 on Hovered and 16.5:1 on Pressed.
- Each class sets its own colors in its code, as defaults (see Data rules in `CLAUDE.md`): `APropositionBlock` in its constructor; `AOperatorBlock` in `OnConstruction`, since it chooses between gray and blue by its token, which arrives through `SetData` during the deferred spawn, after the constructor; `ABlock` the black text, on its label's field in its constructor (see `Docs/Data.md`), and the outline's two colors, through `GetOutlineColor`; `UBlockBoard` the color of its background; and `UIndicators` the colors of its background and of its texts, in its constructor, and its circles' colors through `ChangeColor`, on each answer it shows (see `Docs/Indicators.md`). `URoundedBackgroundComponent` still has two ways to set a color (see Pending in `CLAUDE.md`).
- The operators' blue is close to the indicators' cyan, so the indicators do not rely on color alone: each circle also shows a symbol (see `Docs/Indicators.md`).
- The blocks' text is bold, from DejaVu Sans Condensed Bold (see `Docs/Font.md`). Each block class decides its label size through `GetLabelSize`, a virtual method of `ABlock` (Template Method): half the block's height for propositions and 0.7 of it for operators, so that the symbols fill their circles. `ABlock::Height` is protected for that: it is a `float`, so hiding it behind a ratio returned by each subclass, as `GetRowHeightRatio` does in the manager, would add methods while hiding no class (see Engineering principles in `CLAUDE.md`).
- Post-processing changed the blocks' unlit colors: the exposure (Auto Exposure Bias 1) doubled them, the filmic tone curve made them pastel, bloom drew a halo that blurred the text, Local Exposure's contrast (0.8 by default since 5.5) brightened the dark table, more where it was far from the blocks, and the vignette (0.4 by default) darkened the screen from the center, to about 60% at the corners.
- Project Settings (`DefaultEngine.ini`) turns bloom off and sets Auto Exposure Bias to 0 and Local Exposure's Highlight Contrast Scale and Shadow Contrast Scale to 1. It has no defaults for the tone curve, the gamut or the vignette, so the camera sets them in its `PostProcessSettings` (`ACameraPawn::SetPostProcess`): Tone Curve Amount 0, Expand Gamut 0 and Vignette Intensity 0, each with its `bOverride_` flag. A field left unchecked in a camera or a Post Process Volume does not turn a feature off: the engine's default applies.
- Lumen, Unreal's dynamic global illumination and reflections, which the project template turned on, is off in Project Settings (`DefaultEngine.ini`): Dynamic Global Illumination Method and Reflection Method are None (`r.DynamicGlobalIlluminationMethod=0` and `r.ReflectionMethod=0`). Nothing on the table uses light, since its materials are unlit and it has no light, and Lumen's work for orthographic cameras showed up in the log as a performance warning of the engine's own, about `r.Lumen.Ortho.OverrideMeshDFTraceDistances`. Hardware ray tracing and mesh distance fields, which served only Lumen and the path tracer, are still on (see Pending in `CLAUDE.md`).
- Anti-aliasing is TSR (`r.AntiAliasingMethod=4`). `r.MSAACount=4`, also in `DefaultEngine.ini`, does nothing, since MSAA needs forward shading.
- UMG skips post-processing, so the same color looks darker in the panel than in the world, and the panel's colors were chosen by eye.
- Set aside by the author's decision: the screen does not show exactly the colors the code sets. Measured in a screenshot, everything in the world was about 19% brighter in linear values than in the code (the table about #1E2228 instead of #1B1F25, the blue blocks in the same ratio), probably because of the exposure; not investigated.
- The table is a Plane in `Main`: the engine's 1 m plane, at scale 1, at Z −10, with no collision, named `Table`, and with `MI_Table` (#1B1F25), an instance of `M_Block`, in `Content/Table/`. It covers windows from 1:2 to 32:9. By the author's decision, it is an exception to the data rules and to `Main` holding no pieces, since it is only a mesh with a color. `Main` no longer has a DirectionalLight or a SkyAtmosphere, which unlit materials do not need. `ABackgroundTarget` still takes the presses on empty space, with its box between the blocks and the table (see `Docs/Input.md` and `Docs/Scale.md`); it may later be renamed (such as `ATable`) and take the table's mesh, replacing the box.
- Every board has a background, by the author's decision: a `URoundedBackgroundComponent` that `UBlockBoard` holds, 0.1 block height below the blocks, with no collision (see `Docs/Layout.md`). Its size is fixed: the rows' maximum width plus a margin of 0.3 block height on each side, by a number of rows that each subclass of the manager gives through `GetBackgroundRowCount` (see `Docs/BlocksManager.md`): 6 in the formula, 13 in the operator palette and 2 in the proposition palette. Its corners have a fixed radius of 0.4 block height (see `Docs/Background.md`).
- The formula's origin is at X = 1.6 cm, so that its background ends where the operator palette's does. On 16:9 and wider windows, the camera cuts both at the bottom, and the operator palette's also at the top, since it is taller than the 28 cm always shown. The operator palette's background stands 0.3 cm from the formula's and from the proposition palette's, which the author accepts in place of the 1 cm agreed before.
- The outline: the block's `Width` and `Height` are its outer size, the outline's, and its background is smaller by the outline's thickness on each side (0.06 block height, 0.12 cm), so the block takes the same room as before the outline and its text keeps its size. The selection only changes the outline's color, through `URoundedBackgroundComponent::ChangeColor` (see `Docs/Background.md`). The rows place the blocks by `Width`, so neighboring outlines touch, which the author accepts in place of the gap between blocks agreed before. The outline has no collision, so a press on it reaches `ABackgroundTarget` (see `Docs/Input.md`).
- The panel's look lives in `WBP_CommandPanel` (see `Docs/Data.md`). Its buttons use a Rounded Box with a fixed radius of 20 and an outline of width 0 in the fill's color: #39424C when Normal and Disabled, #667488 when Hovered and #1B1F25, the table's color, when Pressed; Slot Padding 6 on each side, and Normal and Pressed Padding 12 × 4. The panel sits 12 from the bottom of the screen and fits its content (Size To Content). Its selection buttons are disabled rather than hidden (see `Docs/Commands.md`).
- Rejected: the orange #E69F00 that the research picked for the propositions (the author prefers yellow); pure yellow #FFFF02 (it outshone the blue); white text on the blocks (1.5:1 to 1.9:1 on their colors); the engine's default post-processing (it changed the unlit colors, as above); a Post Process Volume in `Main` (values in an asset, against the data rules; it was used only before knowing how to set them in code); a darker sheet behind the formula (#232830) and backgrounds that grow with the rows (both dropped by the author while trying the look).
