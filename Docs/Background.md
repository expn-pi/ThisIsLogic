# Background design

- The mesh is a triangle fan: vertex 0 is the center, vertices 1 to 4(n+1) are the outline, and the triangles are (0, i, i+1) plus the closing (0, last, 1). This works because the shape is convex.
- The outline goes bottom-left, bottom-right, top-right, top-left: counter-clockwise on screen, where +X points up and +Y right. Only that order faces the camera (the material is single-sided); the reverse is culled. It looks clockwise only when X and Y are drawn as on paper.
- Each corner has n+1 vertices and every edge turns 90/(n+1) degrees. The vertices sit at half steps around the corner's arc center (r inward from the rectangle corner), at r / cos(half step) from it, so the edges are tangent to the circle of radius r. With n = 0 this is the exact rectangle, and the straight sides always lie on the rectangle's edges, so the size never changes.
- The radius r is either `Roundness` times half the smaller side, so it follows the size, or a fixed radius set with `SetCornerRadius`, for backgrounds whose corners do not follow their size, such as the boards' (see `Docs/Visuals.md`). `Build` and `Resize` both recompute the offset of each outline vertex from its rectangle corner (it depends on n and r). `Build`, called once, creates the vertex array and the material, a dynamic material instance of the base material that the component keeps; `Resize` places the four corners, adds the offsets in place and recreates the mesh section (`BuildMesh`, with `CreateMeshSection_LinearColor`), which also rebuilds the collision used by the click. The vertex array keeps its size: changing n, through `SetCornerSegments` (added for the table, unused for now), needs `Build`.
- The color is the `Color` parameter of that dynamic material. `SetColor` gives it before `Build`, which applies it; `ChangeColor` changes it afterwards, while the game runs, as the blocks' outline does when the selection changes (see `Docs/Visuals.md`). Keeping only `ChangeColor` is in Pending, in `CLAUDE.md`.
- The component's collision comes from its mesh section, and the block's background takes the click through it. Owners whose background takes no presses turn it off: `ABlock` for the outline and `UBlockBoard` for the board's background (see `Docs/Input.md`).
- `check`s: n ≥ 0, 0 ≤ `Roundness` ≤ 1, and r at most half the smaller side, which a fixed radius must also respect.
- Rejected: an actor for the block's background (the click would find the background, not the block); rounding drawn by the material (collision stays rectangular, and it needs UVs); vertices on the circle at the tangent points (no n = 0, duplicate vertices in capsules); one corner list mirrored for the others (mirroring reverses the order, rotation keeps it); scaling the component to resize (stretches the corners and the label); a fixed radius for the blocks (it had to be kept at half the block's `Height` by hand, while `Roundness` follows the size); computing the radius when it is set (the size only arrives in `Build` and `Resize`); `UpdateMeshSection_LinearColor` in `Resize` (in UE5 it updates only the drawing, and the collision keeps the old shape); a separate collision shape on the block, such as a box or a capsule (one more component, an approximate shape, and the background would no longer guarantee that its collision matches its drawing).
